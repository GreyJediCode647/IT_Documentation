Tittle:
Create and manage group, including Microsoft 365 Group and shared mailbox [Microsoft 365 Admin Center]

Purpose:
Create MS365 group and shared mailbox.

Prerequisites:
- Microsoft 365 Business Basic (Licence)
- Global Administrator, Exchange Administrator, Groups Administrator, or Directory Writers (Role)

Procedure:

1. Visit Microsoft 365 Admin Center (admin.microsoft.com).
2. On the Left-hand-side of the screen look for Teams & groups and click on it.
3. Underneath that option click on Active teams and groups.
4. Once you are on the Active teams and group page you will be able to see a tab named as Teams & Microsoft 365 groups, click on that tab.
5. Under that tab you can see an option named as + Add a Microsoft 365 Group, click on that option.
6. That will open up a wizard that will help you create a MS365 group. A 'Set up the basic' page will open up on that page you can Name the group and give a small description for the group. Example: Name: Finance Description: This is a Finace group for New York Location, the group owner is Greg Brown and this contact mail id is greg@woodys.com. Once you have filled in the information click on Next.
7. Now you have to assign owner for this group, click on + Assign owner and select the user that you want to assign this role. After that click on Next.
8. This is the page where we add memnber to the group by clicking on + Add members and selecting the users you want to add to this group. Once that is done click on Next. 
9. On the Edit setting page you are able to assign a group email address (any email send to this group email address will be received by all the memebers of the group). The another option on this page is the Privacy setting you have two option 1. Public : where anyone can be added to this group no need for any aprovel from the group owner. 2. Private: where only group owners can only add users to this group. Select the option that is right for you. After that on the bottom of the paget you have option named Add Microsoft Teams to your group, which enables a Teams for your group. Once you have set your desired settings click on Next.
10. Now you are on the Review and finish adding group page where you can review all the information that you just entered to create the group once you have checked wverything is right click on Create group and the group will be created. 
11. Once the group has been created and the meembers are added you will come to page name {Group name} group created with a green tick mark stating the group has been created successfully. Click on Close to exit the wizard. 

Verifiaction:

1. Once again you land back on Active teams and groups page, on the Right-hand-side of the page you can find a search tab to look for your group that you just created to confirm that it has been created. 
2. Click on the group, you can see the properties and settings of the group that you can change nay time as you chose.
